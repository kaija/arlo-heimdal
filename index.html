Protection has to respect the person it protects.

Every product decision starts with the account owner’s dignity, privacy, and ability to say no.

01 / Read-only

It never sends, replies, reacts, or marks messages as read.

Heimdal is there to notice and explain. It does not speak or act as someone else.

02 / Visible by design

There are no hidden connections.

The protected person knows Heimdal is active and can disconnect it from the platform’s own linked-device controls.

03 / Private by default

Analysis and visibility are not the same thing.

The system may need to inspect messages for risk; a guardian receives only the alert context the person agreed to share.

04 / Human-led

An alert is a reason to check in, not a verdict.

Heimdal shows its confidence and evidence so families can decide what to do with care.

Built to be helpful without becoming intrusive.

Guardrails are part of the service, not a setting buried in the fine print.

Visible access levels

The person being protected selects how much alert context a guardian can receive. More access always requires renewed consent.

Local-first screening

Fast, predictable checks happen close to home. Only the necessary message fragments move to deeper analysis.

Scam links stay closed

Links extracted from suspicious messages are never opened. An alert makes the risk visible without giving a scammer a signal back.
